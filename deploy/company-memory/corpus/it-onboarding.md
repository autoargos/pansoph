allow_groups: everyone

Marker: IT-ONBOARD-01 PUBLIC.

New laptops are enrolled in device management on day one. Enable full disk encryption and the password manager before accessing client systems. VPN access is requested through the IT Ops queue and is granted per project.

For access problems, open an IT Ops ticket with the system name and the error. Do not share credentials in chat.
