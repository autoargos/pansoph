# DOOR3 IT Support Quick Guide (synthetic demo knowledge)

SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT for the local Company Memory POC. These procedures, names, hours,
and profiles are invented for the demo. This is not real DOOR3 IT guidance.

## Contacting IT Support

- Post in the internal support channel `#it-help-demo` (fictional), or open a ticket in the
  demo service desk queue `ITS-DEMO`.
- Demo support hours: Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 18:00 Eastern. Outside those hours, page the
  demo on call rotation for outages only.

## Standard laptop onboarding (demo)

1. Collect the laptop and sign the demo asset form.
2. Sign in with your company account and enrol a passkey.
3. Let the device management agent install the baseline apps (about 20 minutes).
4. Install the VPN client and import the demo VPN profile.
5. Confirm disk encryption is on in the security settings.

## VPN troubleshooting checklist (demo)

The internal demo VPN profile is called NORTHSTAR.

1. Check that you are on the NORTHSTAR profile, not an old one.
2. Sign out of the VPN client and back in to refresh the certificate.
3. Switch networks (a hotel or guest network may block the VPN port).
4. Still failing: post the client log in `#it-help-demo` with the time it failed.

## Password reset escalation (demo)

1. Use self service reset first.
2. If you are locked out of self service, ask IT Support in `#it-help-demo`; they verify your
   identity with your manager before any reset.
3. IT Support never asks for your current password.
