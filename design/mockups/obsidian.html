What is our on-call rotation policy for the platform team, and who owns incident escalation?

The platform team runs a weekly on-call rotation of five engineers, handed off every Monday at 10:00 UTC.[1] Primary on-call acknowledges within 5 minutes; unacknowledged pages auto-escalate to the secondary, then to the engineering manager.[2]

Incident escalation is owned by the Reliability group. Sev-1 incidents page leadership automatically and open a war room in Slack.[3]
