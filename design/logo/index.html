pansoph · brand exploration

Four logo concepts

pansoph (from pansophy, all-encompassing wisdom) is an open-source, access-aware knowledge platform; the app is lens. Each concept below is shown as a standalone mark, a horizontal lockup, a row of favicon sizes, and a single-color version, on both a light and a dark swatch. All artwork is pure vector SVG with no external font dependency.

ink  #12161F accent  #1FA6A0 paper  #F4F4F5

Concept 01

aperture

A minimal iris that also reads as a lowercase p: shutter blades spiral around an open pupil, with a stem dropping from the ring. The meaning is baked into the geometry. You see exactly what you are allowed to see (access-aware), and it ties directly to the lens app.

Concept 02

constellation

Linked nodes form a small graph in the silhouette of a lowercase p, a spine of nodes for the stem and a closed loop for the bowl. Many sources unified into one access-controlled knowledge graph; the single accented node is the point of governed access.

Concept 03

wordmark

A refined lowercase geometric wordmark with one clever detail: the o becomes a lens/aperture, quietly echoing Concept 01 and the lens app. Timeless and typographic. The compact aperture tile doubles as an app icon when a symbol is needed.

Letterforms are drawn from a geometric grotesque (Futura Medium) and converted to vector paths, so no font is referenced inside the SVG.

Concept 04

owl

An abstract owl / eye-of-wisdom monogram. Two eyes double as apertures, one accented to signal seeing with permission; the brow is a single continuous sweep. Memorable and characterful, a touch more playful than the others.
